Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

1. About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how FlexiSpace collects, holds, uses, discloses and transfers personal information when providing software, booking, facility management, storage management, payment, payout, support and related services.

FlexiSpace is a software and payments product focused on the storage and sports court management verticals. This Privacy Policy should be read together with the applicable merchant customer terms, payment terms, direct debit or recurring payment disclosures, and any notices shown at sign-up, checkout, payment setup or account creation.

2. Who we are

FlexiSpace (ABN 31 106 055 181) is a software and payments platform for merchants operating in the storage and sports court management verticals.

In this Privacy Policy, Merchant means the storage operator, sports court operator, venue, facility owner or other business providing the goods or services to the customer.

In general, the merchant is responsible for the underlying goods or services, including pricing, service delivery, cancellation rules and refund entitlement, while FlexiSpace is responsible for the software, payment-processing functions, payment administration and related operational support it performs.

3. Who this Privacy Policy applies to

  • customers who book courts, hire facilities, rent storage units or otherwise use a merchant's services through FlexiSpace;
  • users who make one-off card payments, recurring card payments, stored-credential payments or direct debit payments through FlexiSpace;
  • people who create or manage customer accounts;
  • authorised users, staff, managers, operators and representatives of merchants;
  • players, participants, guests, emergency contacts, secondary contacts, occupiers and other persons whose details are entered into FlexiSpace in connection with a booking or storage arrangement; and
  • website visitors, support contacts and enquiry submitters.

4. What personal information we collect

The personal information we collect depends on how FlexiSpace is used and which product features are enabled.

  • registration and login information, including first name, last name, email address and phone number;
  • additional information required by a merchant, such as vehicle model, registration number, date of birth, secondary contact details and other custom fields configured by the merchant;
  • account and profile details, such as usernames, passwords, preferences, merchant account settings, permissions and account history;
  • booking and facility information, such as booking dates, times, venue or court selections, participant names, attendance records, waitlist information and usage history;
  • storage information, such as storage site, unit number, tenancy or licence details, move-in and move-out dates, occupier details, account status, arrears status, suspension status and communications about the storage arrangement;
  • payment and financial details, such as card details, bank account details, direct debit details, payment method tokens, transaction history, payment status, payout information and billing records;
  • saved payment-method preferences, including tokenised payment references and truncated payment identifiers displayed in the user account or checkout flow;
  • payment arrangement information, such as recurring amounts, due dates, payment frequency, retry status, failed or dishonoured payments, mandate or authority records, notices, variations and cancellation records;
  • records of customer consent, including acceptance of agreements, acknowledgements, acceptance timestamps, e-signatures or equivalent electronic acceptance records, and the version of terms accepted;
  • customer support and dispute records, such as emails, phone notes, support tickets, chat logs, complaints and dispute records; and
  • technical and usage information, such as IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, cookies, event logs, session logs and audit trail data.

5. How we collect personal information

  • directly from you when you create an account, make a booking, set up a payment method, enter into a payment arrangement, submit a form, contact support or otherwise interact with FlexiSpace;
  • from the merchant when the merchant creates or manages customer records, bookings, storage arrangements, access rights, billing records or payment arrangements;
  • from banks, payment processors, card schemes, gateways, acquiring partners, direct debit service providers and financial institutions in connection with payment processing and status updates;
  • from fraud prevention, identity verification, risk, compliance, analytics, hosting and security providers;
  • automatically through our websites, apps, payment pages, APIs, systems and cookies; and
  • from publicly available sources where lawful and reasonable.

6. Why we collect, use and disclose personal information

  • providing the FlexiSpace platform and related services;
  • enabling merchants to manage bookings, schedules, facilities, storage units, occupancies and customer interactions;
  • processing customer payments, refunds, chargebacks, direct debits, recurring charges and failed payments;
  • creating, administering and enforcing payment arrangements;
  • administering saved payment methods and pre-populated checkout experiences where a customer has chosen to save a payment method;
  • managing portal-based payment and subscription maintenance, including payment status, subscription status, cancellation requests and related account servicing;
  • communicating about bookings, payment setup, failed payments, reminders, disputes, support matters or account issues;
  • verifying identity and helping detect fraud, misuse, unauthorised access and suspicious transactions;
  • assessing whether payment retries, fraud controls or additional authentication steps are appropriate;
  • operating risk-based transaction monitoring and authentication controls, which may vary by merchant, product type, payment method, transaction amount, fraud indicators or other risk factors;
  • managing user authentication, permissions, audit logs and platform security;
  • reconciling payments, reporting, settlements and payouts; and
  • complying with legal, regulatory, contractual, tax, audit, risk and reporting obligations.

7. Storage-specific processing

Where FlexiSpace is used in the storage vertical, we may collect, use and disclose personal information to create and manage customer and occupier records, administer storage rentals, licences or occupancy arrangements, manage billing, arrears, retries, recurring card payments and direct debits, and support operational servicing.

We may also use payment status, retry history, subscription status, cancellation data and support history to administer recurring payments, manage overdue balances, communicate about access impacts and provide customer or merchant support.

8. Sports court-specific processing

Where FlexiSpace is used in the sports court management vertical, we may collect, use and disclose personal information to create and manage customer profiles and bookings, manage court availability, booking dates, times and participant information, and process checkout-based card payments.

We may also use booking status, payment status, attendance information, cancellation requests, refund-related decisions and support records to administer one-off bookings, customer support and merchant workflows.

9. Payment methods and payment arrangements

Depending on the merchant's configuration and the product workflow, FlexiSpace may support customer-initiated card payments, stored-credential or recurring card payments, including merchant-initiated transaction arrangements, and direct debit arrangements.

If you enter into a payment arrangement through FlexiSpace, we may collect and use personal information to create, administer and enforce that arrangement. A payment arrangement may include the amount payable, payment method, billing frequency, due dates, account or reference number, transaction history, failed, dishonoured, retried or disputed payments, cancellation or variation records and communications relating to the arrangement.

If you choose to save a payment method for future use, FlexiSpace may store a token or other secure reference rather than full raw credentials, and may show a truncated payment identifier back to you in your account or checkout flow.

We may retain records relating to a payment arrangement, including sign-up screens, acceptance timestamps, payment authorisations, notices, transaction history, updater events, customer communications, cancellation requests and retry outcomes for compliance, fraud prevention, audits, operational support, dispute resolution and chargeback defence.

10. Merchant and platform role allocation

In general, the merchant is responsible for the underlying goods or services, including the offer, pricing, service delivery, cancellation rules and refund entitlement.

FlexiSpace is responsible for the software, payment-processing functions, payment administration and related operational support it performs. FlexiSpace does not ordinarily decide whether a customer is entitled to a refund for the merchant's goods or services, except where required by law or where FlexiSpace elects to do so under a specific arrangement.

11. Disclosure and transfer of personal information

We may disclose or transfer personal information to:

  • merchants using FlexiSpace;
  • storage operators, sports court operators, venue operators, landlords, owners, agents, property managers or other organisations connected with the relevant service;
  • banks, financial institutions, payment processors, gateways, acquiring partners, direct debit providers, card schemes and wallet providers;
  • fraud prevention, identity verification, compliance, hosting, analytics, communications, customer support and security providers;
  • professional advisers, insurers, auditors and contractors; and
  • regulators, courts, tribunals, law enforcement bodies and government agencies where required or permitted by law.

We may also transfer, store, process or disclose your personal information outside Australia where our service providers, infrastructure or support operations are located overseas, or where cross-border processing is reasonably necessary to provide our services.

12. Overseas disclosure

Some of our service providers or technology systems may be located outside Australia, or may store or process personal information overseas. Where we disclose or transfer personal information overseas, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the recipient handles the information in a manner consistent with applicable privacy requirements, unless an exception applies.

13. How we hold and protect personal information

We hold personal information in electronic systems, cloud platforms, databases, logs, support tools, payment systems, storage and booking records and, in limited cases, physical records.

  • access controls and role-based permissions;
  • encryption and secure transmission methods;
  • audit logging and monitoring;
  • tokenisation or secure credential storage for payment methods where supported;
  • network, application and infrastructure security controls;
  • staff training and internal policies; and
  • vendor due diligence and contractual safeguards.

14. Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to provide the FlexiSpace platform and related services, maintain booking, storage, payment, payout and operational records, maintain records of payment arrangements and payment authorisations, investigate disputes, complaints, chargebacks and security incidents, comply with legal and regulatory obligations, and enforce our rights and agreements.

15. Access and correction

You may request access to the personal information we hold about you, and you may ask us to correct personal information that is inaccurate, out of date, incomplete, irrelevant or misleading. We will respond within a reasonable time and in accordance with applicable law.

16. Cookies, analytics and website data

Our websites, apps, portals and payment pages may use cookies and similar technologies to authenticate sessions, remember preferences, improve functionality, understand usage and performance, support analytics, fraud prevention and security, and assist with product improvement. You can manage cookie settings through your browser, although some features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

17. Direct marketing

We may use your personal information to send service-related communications, such as booking reminders, payment receipts, failed payment notices, security alerts, account notices and updates about your payment arrangement or FlexiSpace account. These are generally not marketing communications.

Where permitted by law, we may also send promotional communications about our products or services. You can opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link or contacting us.

18. Complaints

If you have a complaint about how we have handled your personal information, please contact us using the details below and provide enough information for us to understand and investigate your concern. We will acknowledge your complaint and seek to respond within a reasonable time.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may be able to make a complaint to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.
